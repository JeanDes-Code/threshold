# The model names Stakes and Factors; the Ruleset sets difficulty

A Decision Proposal never contains a numeric difficulty. The GM model returns the Stakes and the Factors drawn from the fiction. The Ruleset turns them into a difficulty and a Roll. On 2026-11-08 we asked two large models for a 1-10 difficulty on a public insult to a vindictive magistrate. Both answered 1 on most runs, even though the output matched the schema. A JSON schema checks shape, not judgment. Keeping numbers in the Ruleset makes Checks reproducible and lets us replace the Ruleset without retraining or re-prompting the model.
