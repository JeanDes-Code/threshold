The Ashford Road - campaign notes

Ashford is a small hill village above a lake. The Lantern Inn stands in Ashford, beside the old well. The mill house is part of Ashford, down by the stream. The chapel is inside Ashford's walls. The ferry landing is at the foot of the hill, by the lake.

The ferry carries travellers over to Harrowmere. Harrowmere is a fishing town on the far shore of the lake.

Bran Holt (38) is the player character, a carter hauling grain to Harrowmere. He travels with his wife, Wren Holt (36), a weaver. They are both adults and have a room at the Lantern Inn. Tonight they are at the Lantern Inn.

Ada Miller (40) keeps the mill house. Her daughter, Pip Miller, is 10 years old and lives with her at the mill house.

Old Tamsin (70) rings the chapel bell. A stranger in Harrowmere is waiting for Bran's grain.
