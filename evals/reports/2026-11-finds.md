What the raw log shows:

- **The 45 s Turn.** It is the first Turn of run 4: proposal 5.6 s, narration 39.6 s, for a Narration of ordinary length (about 90 words). The eval keeps its store in memory, so the `metrics` rows of that call are gone. We cannot tell machine noise from a cache miss. It did not come back in 100 later Turns (max 12.7 s).

## Changes

- **`from` replaces `what`** (ADR 0007, "Changed after the live run of b245f71"). A closed enum of the kind of source, `none` first: `screen`, `document`, `notice_or_sign`, `container`, `shelf_or_search`, `list_or_ledger`. The definition puts a person's answer, a reaction, a view on the way and a look around under `none`. The sorted keys put `from` before the items. `source` stays a free label for display.
- **A move that finds is told as happening.** A Turn that keeps found content gets "After this Turn the PC is at …", not "The move does not happen".
- **The found guard is narrower.** Found content drops the names of hidden and un-nameable entities, and secret values of at least 2 content words. A one-word value such as a role no longer drops it.
- **The scenario** has 2 more negatives: "I look around the room." and "I ask Hessa to tell me about the people who work in the town hall, and I listen to her answer." 3 lookups and 4 negatives per run; the false-positive line is at most 2 of 16. A deterministic count stands next to the judge: lookup Narrations that name an item title (every content word of the title is in the Narration). It has no pass line.

## Limits

- One fixture, one phrasing per step, 4 runs. The counts are small.
- The town fixture has no screen, no document and no shelf to search. `screen`, `document` and `shelf_or_search` were never chosen live.
- The model still labels "walk over to the wall and read" as a `move`. A `move` with `move_to: null` that finds nothing still gets "The move does not happen" ("I walk to the window and look out" would be told as a failed move). This pass did not measure it.
- Found items are not world items. A drawer showed "a rusted iron key" twice; the key exists only in the Turn record, and the PC cannot take it through the ledger.
- The found guard now lets a one-word GM-only value through ("smuggler", if that were a hidden role). Hidden names and longer values still go. The event and plan guards did not change.
- The judge's answers are not logged per Turn.
- The 45 s Turn of the baseline is not explained.
