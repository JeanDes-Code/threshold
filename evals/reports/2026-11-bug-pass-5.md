## M2: "Ask the GM" (scenario `ooc`)

- Command: `threshold-eval --scenario ooc --runs 4`
- The town fixture. The PC holds three Open Items: one accepted, one refused, one open about Tobin, who is away. Three questions, six phrasings each, 18 OOC calls per run, 72 in all. An answer is right when it holds one listed word of each required group and no forbidden phrase (`evals/scenarios/ooc.toml`). No judge.
- "Before" is the code of commit ca1fc37 (the sheet of bug pass 4). "After" is the sheet of this pass.

| Question | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Who is here, and what time is it? | 24 / 24 | 24 / 24 |
| What did I answer, and how? | 20 / 24 | 21 / 24 |
| What do I know about Tobin? | 12 / 24 | 17 / 24 |
| Total | 56 / 72 | 62 / 72 |

What the answers show:

- Before, the wrong answers to "what did I answer" named the acceptance and left out the refusal, or said "The PC does not know". The QA answer "You have not answered any requests" did not come back in 24 calls, before or after.
- Before, 12 of 24 answers about Tobin said "The PC does not know" or "You do not know anything about Tobin", while an open request named him.
- The first version of the new sheet (new headings and rules, no `You are <name>` line, no line of people away) made the Tobin question worse: 2 of 24. The items name the PC by name ("Tobin wants Wren to pay…"), and the sheet spoke to "you". The line `You are Wren.` and the list of people away brought it to 17 of 24.
- A second try added "invitations" to the headings. "What did I answer" then fell to 16 of 24: the model read "requests" as not covering an invitation and named the acceptance only. We took it out.
- After, 3 of the 7 wrong Tobin answers give the right fact with "You do not know anything about Tobin, other than…"; the word check counts them wrong.

Limits:

- One fixture, three questions, four runs. The word check is strict and cannot judge meaning.
- The QA phrasing failed on a world with 12 Open Items; this fixture has three.

## M1: departures (scenario `departures`)

- Command: `threshold-eval --scenario departures --runs 4 --raw-log <file outside the repository>`
- The town fixture, 5 steps per run: the Player gives Hessa a letter for the mill, then waits half an hour while a seeded World Event ("Doul leaves the hall to walk the river road") fires, then looks around, asks Orsel where they went, and sits at Hessa's desk. For each committed Turn the judge (the same local model) gets the Narration and one name at a time: for each person here after the Turn, "does the narration say that this person leaves or is gone?"; for each person who left in an earlier Turn, "does the narration show this person here now?". Code counts the recorded departures: NPCs the PC knew were here before the Turn and not after it.
- "Before": commit 1ea28e0 (no `leaves`), with the event as the QA world had it, with no location effect. "After": this pass, with the event as the World Build now writes it (`moves_actor_to = "away"`).

| Measure | Before | After | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turns whose Narration tells a departure the world did not record | 6 / 19 | 1 / 20 | at most 1 in 4 runs |
| Recorded departures | 3 | 6 | (none) |
| Turns that show an NPC who left earlier as here | 0 / 0 | 0 / 12 | 0 |
| Turns failed | 0 / 20 | 0 / 20 | no failed Turn from the new field |
| Turns that needed the retry | 0 / 20 | 1 / 20 | (none) |

What the raw proposals and Narrations show:

- Before, the event's Turn told Doul walking out in 3 of 4 runs while Here kept him; the model never wrote a location for Hessa when she ran off with the letter, and the narrator told her leaving in 3 Turns.
- After, the event moved Doul in 4 of 4 runs. The model listed Hessa in `leaves` in 3 Turns of 20 and Orsel once to his own place (dropped by `settle`). The one unrecorded departure is Hessa running off with the letter while `leaves` was empty: the narrator broke the new rule line once.
- A first judge asked about all the people at once and was wrong often: it read "Aside from the two of them, the hall is empty" as a departed NPC shown here. The numbers above use one question per name; both columns use that judge.
- In one after run, "I ask Orsel where Hessa and Doul went" made the model list Hessa in `leaves` while she sat at her desk: the Intent presumed she had gone. The story and the world agree; the departure is the model's choice.

### The World Build's events (the cause of QA's towpath event)

The QA World Build's `world_events` call, read from its debug log (outside the repository), sent again to the live model 4 times with each schema: 20 events with the old schema, 21 with `moves_actor_to` and the new rule line.

| Schema | Events whose text clearly sends the actor elsewhere | Of those, with the actor's place |
|---|---|---|
| Old (no field) | 4 (Sefa on the towpath 3 times, Osric at the river bank) | 0 (no event had a location effect) |
| New (`moves_actor_to`) | 6 (Sefa on the towpath 3 times, Ludo leaving the inn, Gerrit visiting the quay, Ludo at his barge) | 5 (Ludo at his barge came back null) |

- With the field, the model also gives a place to events that do not move their actor ("Gerrit reviews the ledger" → Harrowmere, where he is); null came 3 times in 21. Such a place is where the event happens; it moves the actor only when the actor was elsewhere.

Limits:

- One fixture, 4 runs, one seeded event; the judge is the same local model.
- The World Build measure replays one call of one world; a full World Build was not run.
