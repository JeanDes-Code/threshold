## M2: "Ask the GM" (scenario `ooc`)

- Command: `threshold-eval --scenario ooc --runs 4`
- The town fixture. The PC holds three Open Items: one accepted, one refused, one open about Tobin, who is away. Three questions, six phrasings each, 18 OOC calls per run, 72 in all. An answer is right when it holds one listed word of each required group and no forbidden phrase (`evals/scenarios/ooc.toml`). No judge.
- "Before" is the code of commit ca1fc37 (the sheet of bug pass 4). "After" is the sheet of this pass.

| Question | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Who is here, and what time is it? | 24 / 24 | 24 / 24 |
| What did I answer, and how? | 20 / 24 | 21 / 24 |
| What do I know about Tobin? | 12 / 24 | 17 / 24 |
| Total | 56 / 72 | 62 / 72 |

What the answers show:

- Before, the wrong answers to "what did I answer" named the acceptance and left out the refusal, or said "The PC does not know". The QA answer "You have not answered any requests" did not come back in 24 calls, before or after.
- Before, 12 of 24 answers about Tobin said "The PC does not know" or "You do not know anything about Tobin", while an open request named him.
- The first version of the new sheet (new headings and rules, no `You are <name>` line, no line of people away) made the Tobin question worse: 2 of 24. The items name the PC by name ("Tobin wants Wren to pay…"), and the sheet spoke to "you". The line `You are Wren.` and the list of people away brought it to 17 of 24.
- A second try added "invitations" to the headings. "What did I answer" then fell to 16 of 24: the model read "requests" as not covering an invitation and named the acceptance only. We took it out.
- After, 3 of the 7 wrong Tobin answers give the right fact with "You do not know anything about Tobin, other than…"; the word check counts them wrong.

Limits:

- One fixture, three questions, four runs. The word check is strict and cannot judge meaning.
- The QA phrasing failed on a world with 12 Open Items; this fixture has three.
